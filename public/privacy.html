Privacy Policy

Last updated · October 2026

BareKeep does not collect or upload any data about you or your usage: no telemetry, no crash reporting, no account system.

1. What we collect

To activate, verify or deactivate Pro, BareKeep sends the license key and a device label (your Mac’s name plus a short code derived from a hardware identifier) to Dodo Payments over HTTPS. Nothing else is sent to us or to the payment provider.

BareKeep has no accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting and no servers of its own. Its other request to us is the update check described below.

2. What we don’t collect

BareKeep does not collect or receive your repositories, your access tokens, your mirror list, your backup servers, or how you use the app. Everything runs on your Mac and on the servers you chose.

Logs stay on your Mac. Exporting diagnostics creates a file that you send yourself; tokens are removed from it. It does include the mirror list (repository addresses and local paths), so check it before you share it.

3. Third-party services

  • The Git servers you configure: GitHub, GitLab, Gitea, Forgejo or any https Git server, which the system git fetches from and pushes to. A token is sent only to the host it was created for, over https. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • Platform APIs: when you add a self-hosted instance, BareKeep probes the server kind without credentials (only the version endpoint is requested). A backup target’s connection test and automatic repository creation call that platform’s API.
  • dl.barekeep.com: to check for a new version of BareKeep, once a day unless you turn off the daily check, and when you ask. The request carries the app name and version, the update framework’s version and your IP address, and no system profile. An update is downloaded only after you confirm it, and installed only if its Apple signature and BareKeep’s EdDSA signature both verify.
  • Dodo Payments: our payment provider and the merchant of record. At checkout Dodo collects your name, email address and billing details under its own privacy policy, and handles invoices, taxes and refunds. BareKeep does not receive your payment details.
  • This website: static, with no analytics and no cookies. It and the downloads are served by Cloudflare, which keeps standard access logs.
  • Your browser: links such as “Open on …” are opened by the system browser.

4. Data storage

  • Your Pro license key is kept in your Mac’s keychain, as are access tokens.
  • Access tokens are kept only in your Mac’s keychain, one entry per source token and per backup target token. They are never written to files or logs and never appear in command-line arguments or environment variables; they are handed to the git subprocess through a pipe.
  • The mirror list, backup targets and run history are JSON files in ~/Library/Application Support/BareKeep; they contain no tokens. Logs are in ~/Library/Logs/BareKeep, with secrets removed before they are written.
  • Mirror repositories are stored in the folder you specify.

5. Your rights and control

You can deactivate your license in Settings › License at any time. Deleting a mirror or removing a backup target deletes its keychain entry. After uninstalling BareKeep you can delete the folders and keychain entries above.

The developer can see your purchase record (name, email address, payment ID) in the Dodo dashboard, only to give support and process refunds. To ask what we hold, or to have it corrected or deleted, email support@barekeep.com; we reply within 30 days. If you are in the EU or the EEA, you also have the rights the GDPR gives you, including the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.

Questions about BareKeep, a purchase or a refund? Email support@barekeep.com.
