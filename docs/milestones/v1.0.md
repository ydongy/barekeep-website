# v1.0 Launch

> Issue: None · Requirement: `docs/content.md#pages` · Screens: None · Status: in progress

## Goal

A visitor can learn what BareKeep is and why to trust it, download it, see the update history, and read the
legal texts in their own language; the payment provider and the app find every page and feed they need at its
stable path.

## Scope

- The baseline already in place: home page with the download and pricing sections, FAQ, changelog, Privacy
  Policy, Terms of Service and Refund Policy in nine languages, the footer with the support address, the
  download button fed by `https://dl.barekeep.com/latest.json`, a strict Content-Security-Policy, and the
  ydongy's generator and checks ([0005](../adr/0005-sitekit-vendored.md)).
- Terms of Service that match the app's `LICENSE` section by section, and a contact line under every legal page.
- The pricing table with a visible price row and the buy button, once the Dodo Payments product exists.
- Real screenshots of the shipped app on the home page.

## Non-goals

- Accounts, a blog, a documentation site, analytics or cookies ([0002](../adr/0002-no-analytics-no-cookies.md)).
- Redirects or alias pages ([0003](../adr/0003-one-address-per-page.md)).
- A contact form; the support address is the only channel.

## Decisions

- 1.0 is paid: a free plan of up to 3 mirrors and a one-time Pro purchase with a license key. Until the price is
  published the Pricing section says so; the table with a price row and the buy button are added when the payment
  product exists, because the payment provider's review needs a visible price.
- The site keeps reading `latest.json` from the download host, which the app's release script writes; the
  appcast carries no checksum or release notes.
- The minimum macOS shown everywhere is 26.0, the app's deployment target; it is a fact in the table in
  [content.md](../content.md#facts).
- Page copy is English first and translated; legal pages and the FAQ carry the translation notice.

## Tests

- `check_i18n.py`: every language matches English. `check_site.py`: pages, links, anchors and the policy.
  `check_facts.py`: the facts table against every language. `check-docs.py` in CI for the documents.
- By hand before launch: the home page in light and dark and at a narrow width; the download button against a
  real `latest.json`; a purchase and a refund flow end to end; mail sent to the support address arrives.

## Acceptance checklist

- [ ] The Cloudflare project `barekeep` exists, is bound to `barekeep.com` and deploys `main`; `workers.dev` and
      preview URLs are off.
- [ ] The support mailbox `support@barekeep.com` exists and receives mail.
- [ ] The maintainer has approved the final text of the Terms of Service, the Privacy Policy and the Refund
      Policy (legal review if wanted).
- [ ] The Dodo Payments product exists; the Pricing section shows the Free/Pro table with a visible price row and
      the buy button, and `scripts/check_facts.py` checks the price in every language.
- [ ] The home page shows real screenshots of the shipped app, not mockups.
- [ ] Every claim in the FAQ is re-checked against the shipped app.
- [ ] The download button shows the version, minimum system and SHA-256 from the live `latest.json`; the bucket's
      CORS rule allows `GET` from `https://barekeep.com`.
- [ ] "Signed and notarized, opens without Gatekeeper warnings" is added to the download section only once the
      released build really is.
- [ ] The app's links (`/`, `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/refund`, `/changelog`, `/#pricing`) open the right page in each
      of the nine languages.
- [x] A contact line is on every legal page and the footer.
- [x] The [security walkthrough](v1.0-security.md) is recorded.
